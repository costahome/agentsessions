Make change first-class

Snapshot sprint state at kickoff, then explain added work, owner changes, estimate changes, and iteration moves. Raw current state cannot explain why delivery shifted.

Use capacity, not item counts

Risk should account for OOF, estimates, blocked age, work type, and interrupt load. Seven small items are not the same as seven large ones.

Keep AzDO authoritative

AzDo.AI should summarize, compare, and deep-link. Edits remain in Azure DevOps initially, followed by a fast refresh and a visible “last synced” receipt.
